# 0005 — Ratings and reviews

Type: feature
Plan: P3.1
Design: D6@1

## What

After a visit the client can rate the salon from 1 to 5 and leave a short review; the salon page shows the average.

## Behaviour

- A past booking offers "Rate your visit".
- The salon page shows the average rating and the newest reviews.

## Scope
